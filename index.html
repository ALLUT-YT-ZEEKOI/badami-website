01 Who We Are

Your partner in navigating the complexity of cross-border payments.

We design, integrate, certify, and operationalize the systems that keep money moving — from strategy through go-live and long-term support.

Badami NextGen Solutions (BNGS) helps banks, governments, fintechs, and financial institutions build, connect, and scale modern payment ecosystems. We specialize in the most complex part of payments where domestic systems meet international networks, regulations intersect, and interoperability becomes mission-critical.

With deep expertise across real-time payment systems, cross-border corridors, scheme integration, compliance, certification, and payment technology, our team brings hands-on experience gained from delivering payment infrastructure across multiple markets.

From enabling a bank to connect with a new payment corridor, supporting a fintech through certification, or partnering with governments on national payment initiatives, BNGS serves as a single, accountable partner.
